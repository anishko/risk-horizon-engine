📘 How to Customize

This interactive viewer shows simulated 15-year growth projections for ETFs, Bonds, REITs, and other assets using data from assets.csv. You can easily add your own investments to test how they might perform.

To add your own assets, open assets.csv in any text editor or Excel and add new rows like this:

Name,Type,GrowthRate,RiskBand
BTC,Other,0.25,0.20
TSLA,Stock,0.15,0.10

How it works:

  • Name → A short symbol or name for the asset (e.g., SPY, BTC, TSLA)
  • Type → The category (ETF, Bond, REIT, Stock, Other, etc.)
  • GrowthRate → Expected yearly return as a decimal (0.07 = 7%)
  • RiskBand → Range of fluctuation above or below the growth rate (0.02 = ±2%)

After adding your rows, rerun the Java program to regenerate results.csv, then reload this page to compare your custom assets using the dropdown and starting amount input.

💡 Tips for Finding Real Growth Rates and Risk Bands (for customization)

When you want to test your own assets, here are some easy ways to estimate or look up the numbers.

(1) Growth Rate (Expected Return)

This is the average yearly return for an asset (how much it grows per year on average).

How to find it:

  • Visit Yahoo Finance → search your asset (e.g., SPY, AAPL). Under “Historical Data”, compare the oldest and newest prices for 5 years and calculate (New / Old)^(1/Years) - 1.
  • Or simply Google “average annual return of [asset name]” — sites like Morningstar, Investopedia, or MarketWatch show long-term averages.

(2) Risk Band (Volatility Range)

This shows how much the asset usually fluctuates up or down from its average return.

How to find it:

  • Check “Volatility” or “Standard Deviation” on Yahoo Finance → Statistics tab or on Morningstar.com.
  • If you can’t find data, estimate it:
    • Stable Bonds → 0.01 – 0.02
    • Large ETFs → 0.02 – 0.03
    • REITs → 0.03 – 0.05
    • Crypto or High-Risk Stocks → 0.10 – 0.25
